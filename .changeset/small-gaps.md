- `c.etag()` answers 304 when `If-None-Match` holds the tag as a weak one, in a list, or is `*`, as a proxy that compresses the response sends it.
- A route for `HEAD` runs for a HEAD request. One without its own still finds GET's.
- A time at its `max()` passes when written with zero seconds, such as `17:00:00` for `17:00`.
- In Markdown, an `<ol start={0}>` starts at 0.
- `multipart.data()` keeps a field with an empty name, as `request.formData()` does.
